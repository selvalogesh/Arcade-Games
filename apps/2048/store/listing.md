**Full description** (max 4000):

```
Swipe to slide every tile on the board. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one. Keep merging to build bigger numbers and reach the 2048 tile!

• Simple swipe controls (arrow keys work too)
• Score and best score tracking
• Keep going after 2048 to chase an even higher score
• Your game is saved automatically, so you can pick up where you left off
• Works fully offline
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no permissions

A clean, distraction-free take on the classic sliding number puzzle.
```
