**Full description** (max 4000):

```
Classic paddle table tennis, made for your phone. Drag anywhere on the screen to slide your paddle and return the ball. Hit it off-centre to send it at an angle and get it past the computer. Every hit makes the ball a little faster, so rallies heat up quickly. First to 7 points wins the match!

• Simple drag controls (arrow keys work too)
• Three levels: Easy, Medium and Hard
• Your wins and losses are kept for each level
• Angled shots, side-wall bounces and rallies that speed up
• Pause any time; the game also pauses itself when you leave the app
• Works fully offline
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no permissions

A clean, distraction-free take on the arcade paddle game.
```
