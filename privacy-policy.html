Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy applies to the Arcade Games apps published by selvalogesh on Google Play, including 2048 and Snake, and to the web versions of these games.

Information we collect

None. The games do not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or device information. They contain no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party SDKs, and the Android apps do not request any permissions, including internet access.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, a game may save your current game and best score locally on your device. This data never leaves your device (other than through your own device backups) and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

Because no personal information is collected from anyone, no personal information is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/selvalogesh/Arcade-Games/issues.
